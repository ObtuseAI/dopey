# Daily Paper Operating Playbook

Pre-session: verify data identity, calendar, events, surfaces, Greek state, risk
budget, hazards, system health, and the promotion, evaluator, forward-evidence, and
policy-required anchor trust states. Pre-decision: validate immutable opportunity,
complete genome, embedded control snapshot, NBBO quote, three prices, costs,
defined-risk topology, portfolio exposure, invalidation, management, exit, and reason
not to act. During shadow: append state without widening limits; require an independent
recorder receipt for each decision and settlement and a distinct outcome-source
receipt for every settled outcome. Post-event: score forecast, structure, size, price,
execution, management, compliance, outcome, and luck separately. A missing or
`not_configured` trust dependency blocks the dependent evidence claim without
fallback. The current environment is L0 and paper-only.
