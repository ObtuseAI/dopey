# DOPEY vNext Master Plan Implementation Report

## Outcome

The accelerated DOPEY vNext operating architecture is implemented as a governed, paper-only research system. The 90-day schedule was discarded as requested; the dependency order and evidence laws were retained.

The implementation now performs the complete first high-value operating loop:

1. loads hash-pinned local market history;
2. computes 48 deterministic indicators independently on daily, weekly, and monthly timeframes;
3. creates probabilistic structure and pattern evidence without trade labels;
4. builds governed cross-sectional breadth, leadership, correlation, and fragility context;
5. enriches a 17-dimension shared market world model;
6. assembles a deterministic task-specific research organism;
7. forms bull, bear, neutral, and unknown hypotheses;
8. runs adversarial and downgrade-only shadow review;
9. decomposes and conservatively caps confidence;
10. applies bounded metacognitive, metabolism, and homeostatic control;
11. records append-only forward predictions and memory;
12. grades only eligible forward outcomes and updates probability calibration;
13. evaluates replay candidates through held-out causal and multiple-testing gates;
14. applies autonomous, protected threshold gates independently to symbols, strategies, and symbol-strategy pairs;
15. publishes a source-backed local observatory verified at desktop and mobile widths;
16. runs a governed, resumable cross-sectional batch over the active history manifest;
17. precommits per-agent contribution and remove-one counterfactual predictions;
18. accrues and settles forward evidence through one central append-only ledger.

The new `dopey/` tree contains 139 Python files and approximately 9,050 source lines. It is a reviewed publication candidate; protected future-market evidence gates remain intentionally unresolved.

## Constitutional Result

The vNext layer remains structurally incapable of execution:

- `paper_only=true`;
- `live_blocked=true`;
- broker authority is false;
- order authority is false;
- order-construction authority is false;
- credential access is false;
- self-unlock is unavailable;
- vNext cannot read or write the protected action-readiness decision;
- shadows can only downgrade, veto, request evidence, terminate, or force abstention;
- evolution cannot alter truth, provenance, settlement, execution, readiness, promotion thresholds, or its evaluator;
- simulation and replay are never counted as forward evidence.

No credential, broker connector, order constructor, real-capital allocator, or live-promotion
path was added. The paper-research scheduler now includes the vNext forward lane and a local,
bounded autonomous strategy-research lane. Research activation requires no human review, but
it cannot change action readiness or execution authority.

## Operational Acceleration — Items 1 through 6

### 1. Reliability hardening — complete

- the exit-policy walk-forward grid is content-addressed and checkpoints every completed cell;
- stale or mismatched grids are quarantined instead of reused;
- nightly regression is split into resumable shards with JUnit, durable logs, exact fingerprints,
  and literal `inconclusive_timeout` outcomes;
- the daily settlement command now uses the package module entrypoint;
- the nightly schedule has a six-hour truthful execution envelope rather than a two-hour false ceiling.

### 2. Governed cross-sectional vNext operation — complete awaiting forward settlement

- one market/event time and one frozen cross-sectional context are shared across the batch;
- symbols come from the governed history manifest, not an implicit watchlist;
- every symbol receives point-in-time history proof and a content-addressed resumable checkpoint;
- a rerun is idempotent and cannot duplicate a central-ledger prediction;
- the acceleration scheduler exposes `vnext_forward_research` as a paper-only lane with an
  evidence contract for the canonical latest batch summary.

The governed paper batch completed all 37 requested symbols with one immutable point-in-time
input set and 37 idempotent ledger predictions. Its canonical summary is
`complete_awaiting_forward_settlement`; all 37 predictions remain pending, none are settled, and
the earliest valid settlement date is `2026-07-28`. The completed evidence contract proves
research-lane operation only and cannot increase readiness or promotion authority.

### 3. Validated evidence-gap integrations — complete

- fundamentals and earnings require point-in-time, hash, row, and schema identity;
- option surfaces expose chain statistics and quote quality but explicitly refuse to infer
  dealer inventory from open interest;
- the HYG/IEF/SHY credit proxy is labeled as a relative-return proxy, not OAS or default risk;
- missing licenses, timestamps, rows, hashes, or schemas fail closed as typed data gaps;
- every accepted adapter records limitations and provenance.

### 4. Measurable multi-agent contribution — complete

- specialist effects are deterministic, bounded, and declared before outcomes;
- predictions persist the base hypotheses, contribution map, and each remove-one-agent
  counterfactual;
- the protected evaluator uses forward settlements, multiclass Brier improvement, bootstrap
  intervals, contested truth, symbol floors, and Bonferroni control;
- it can apply protected research-state validation, degradation, quarantine, retirement, and
  rollback transitions automatically; positive activation still requires the complete
  symbol, strategy, and pair matrix.

### 5. Resumable failure recovery — complete

- the daily operation uses a process-owned lease;
- live owners block overlap; corrupt and dead-owner leases are quarantined;
- ownership is verified before release;
- immutable session snapshots are loaded on resume rather than overwritten by newer source data;
- resumed history hashes are reconciled against the frozen cross-sectional context and fail closed
  on source drift;
- zero-range or zero-volume history makes Ease of Movement unavailable instead of terminating a
  real cross-sectional batch;
- batch state, prediction identity, settlement, and nightly validation survive interruption
  without converting partial work into success.

### 6. Honest forward-evidence accrual and settlement — runtime complete; evidence pending

- settlement runs before and after each cross-sectional batch;
- only mature, point-in-time-valid predictions can enter calibration or ablation;
- legacy or incomplete predictions remain append-only but are quarantined from truth claims;
- the batch remains `complete_awaiting_forward_settlement` until market time supplies outcomes;
- no architecture or test can manufacture the missing 96 forward closes, 200 context decisions,
  three-symbol ablation floor, or held-out cohort/regime proof.
