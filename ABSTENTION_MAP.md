# Abstention Map

Abstain on stale, future, crossed, zero-bid, size-less, incomplete, non-NBBO,
unadjusted, or excessively wide quotes; missing Greek or tail exposure; breached
drawdown, loss, margin, ruin, or concentration limits; 0DTE without dedicated
intraday evidence; behavioral drift; unexplained negative-control anomaly; synthetic
profitability claims; or incomplete trial accounting.
