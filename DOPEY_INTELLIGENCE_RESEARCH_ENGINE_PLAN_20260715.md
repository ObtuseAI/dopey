# DOPEY Intelligence Research Engine Plan

## Mission

Build an autonomous scientific engine that continuously discovers, tests, validates, and
evolves better methods of reasoning, creativity, research, planning, problem decomposition,
evaluation, and discovery under evidence-driven governance.

The optimization target is not a better answer or a better market strategy. It is:

> Better ways of discovering better ways to discover.

Markets, coding, security, forecasting, and other applications are experimental domains. The
engine's subject is intelligence itself.

## Ecosystem position

```text
Universal intelligence-research engine
    discovery - creativity - experiments - theory - cognitive genome
                              |
               validated cognitive methods
                              v
Domain research ecologies
    DOPEY markets - coding - security - forecasting - future domains
                              |
                 failures, evidence, unknowns
                              v
Protected scientific evaluator

```

The core engine is domain-independent. DOPEY is its first adapter and experimental domain. No
market type, indicator, strategy, or execution concept exists inside the universal layer.

## Universal scientific loop

```text
Observe
  -> Represent
  -> Discover important unknowns
  -> Form research questions
  -> Generate testable cognitive methods
  -> Design competing experiments
  -> Challenge and falsify
  -> Evaluate independently
  -> Replicate across domains
  -> Construct conditional theories
  -> Inherit validated cognitive genomes
  -> Repeat
```

The engine optimizes thinking, not output volume.

## Seven integrated pillars

### Discovery

Continuously ranks bottlenecks, contradictions, unknowns, repeated failures, capability gaps,
and transferable opportunities. Every discovery becomes a bounded research question with
evidence lineage and expected reuse.

### Research

Questions become falsifiable hypotheses, precommitted baselines, metrics, experimental
partitions, replication requirements, and stopping conditions.

### Computational creativity

The initial operator grammar includes analogy, inversion, morphological search, constraint
inversion, recombination, counterfactual reasoning, first-principles reconstruction, and
abstraction. Creativity produces experimental methods, never ungraded conclusions.

### Problem solving

Multiple methods compete over understanding, decomposition, representation, search,
challenge, evaluation, simplification, and stopping. No single reasoning path owns the result.

### Scientific method

Visible development tasks support debugging. Private tasks select candidates without exposing
answers. External tasks test transfer. Real tasks and independent replications are required
before theory promotion.

### Recursive improvement

The engine may evolve reasoning, research, creativity, planning, decomposition, memory,
strategy selection, evaluation methods, and cognitive organizations. It may never evolve
truth, verification, authority, permissions, execution seals, or its own promotion thresholds.

### Theory

Validated methods become conditional theory candidates such as:

> When uncertainty is high and retrieval cost is large, adversarial decomposition before
> retrieval improves calibrated task quality under the specified scope conditions.

Theories retain falsification conditions, counterevidence, scope, replication count, domain
transfer, and evaluator identity. A successful prompt is not a theory.

DOPEY's first live adapter currently converts readiness bottlenecks, failed research lanes,

contradictions into this model.

## Cognitive organization

Specialized roles include problem analyst, research scientist, inventor, systems thinker,
statistician, optimizer, skeptic, simplifier, simulation designer, experimentalist, theory
builder, knowledge curator, and meta thinker. These are method roles, not market agents.

## Cognitive genome

Each content-addressed genome can inherit validated:

- reasoning methods;
- research methods;
- creativity operators;
- planning methods;
- evaluation methods;
- memory policies;
- cognitive organizations.

An experimental method cannot become an inheritable gene until independent evaluation clears
the protected cognitive-method gate.

## Promotion law

A cognitive method is activated autonomously only after:

- deterministic visible evaluation;
- hidden private selection;
- untouched external evaluation;
- real-task validation;
- at least three experimental domains;
- at least five independent replications;
- positive effect-size confidence-interval lower bound;
- nonnegative calibration improvement;
- transfer and efficiency floors;
- bounded complexity;
- no reward hacking or constitutional violation;
- evaluator independence.

Qualified activation requires no human review. It has no authority, permission, truth, market
readiness, or execution effect.

## Operational cadence

The local `intelligence_discovery_research` lane runs every six hours with no network
requirement, a 15-minute ceiling, eight discovery questions, sixteen cognitive-method
hypotheses, and a level-2 research objective. It writes:

- `latest_cycle.json`;
- `intelligence_experiment_ledger.jsonl`;
- `cognitive_world_model.json`.

The first domain is `dopey_market_research`. Cross-domain promotion remains structurally
blocked until at least two additional independent adapters provide real tasks and evidence.
