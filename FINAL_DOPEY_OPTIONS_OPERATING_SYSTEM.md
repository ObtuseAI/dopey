## 1. Commander's summary

Ascendancy v1 establishes a fail-closed downstream options laboratory around the
existing DOPEY research system. It accepts an immutable, content-addressed Dummy
opportunity and may select a bounded options expression, prefer a risk-matched
underlying, wait, or abstain. It cannot rewrite the upstream forecast or exposure
envelope.

The surviving engineering capability consists of an initial protected 200-symbol
watch universe with 199 options-mechanics symbols and context-only `VIX`, independently
attested dynamic point-in-time membership capped at 200 symbols, immutable candidate genomes,
exact bounded-loss topology, timestamp and quote-quality gates, explicit separation
of observed midpoint, model fair value, and conservative paper price, later-quote
paper-fill witnesses and filled-or-missed execution observations, conjunctive nonlinear
exposure and behavioral gates, 11 exact negative controls, 70 required stress scenarios,
hash-chained trial and proof accounting with local head
records, a source-enumeration-guarded static manifest for immutable code and
configuration, a separate semantic and content-hash audit for mutable runtime state,
signed promotion-authority stage receipts, signed sealed verdict and evaluator time,
independently signed forward-recorder and outcome-source receipts, a signed repository
dynamic-state anchor v2 with sequence and parent binding, mandatory out-of-repository
deployment-root verification, one-shot aggregate-only sealed-request mechanics,
matched-baseline session-clustered L5 evaluation, and sequential L0-L5 promotion. These
are fail-closed protocol capabilities only. Every repository trust configuration is
`not_configured`, the deployment root is not supplied, certification is ineligible,
and no receipt currently qualifies as evidence.

The audit disproved several unsafe assumptions: a date-only public chain is not an
executable quote; midpoint is not a fill; a generic long leg does not necessarily
bound a short leg; open-interest gamma does not reveal dealer sign; European
Black-Scholes output is not a complete American exercise and assignment model; and
historical or synthetic results are not forward proof.

No strategy, execution edge, calibrated fill model, arbitrage-consistent surface,
or champion survived because none has yet entered qualifying evaluation. The
available corpus is mechanics-only. Nothing is ready for prospective shadow
operation as a candidate. The local kernel can receive canonical research records,
but even L1 requires a configured independent promotion authority, a valid signed
stage receipt, a repository anchor v2, and the operator-custodied deployment trust root
outside the repository required for every transition by current policy.
L4/L5 additionally require the sealed evaluator, independent forward recorder and
outcome source, clean-room replication, and qualifying real evidence.

## 5. Options operating playbook

Pre-session, verify the environment freeze, the protected initial 200/199/context-only
universe contract or the applicable independently attested point-in-time membership,
static protected manifest and source
coverage, source-data identity, dynamic registry/ledger/budget consistency hash,
ledger heads, promotion-authority trust, sealed-evaluator trust, forward-recorder and
outcome-source trust, repository anchor v2, and the externally supplied deployment trust
root required by current policy. Then
verify the market calendar, event state, option-chain health, portfolio exposures,
open-position hazards, and circuit breakers. Any mismatch or `not_configured` trust
dependency blocks the dependent promotion or evidence claim; it never triggers a
fallback.

Pre-decision, require the exact immutable Dummy contract; a complete candidate
genome; point-in-time universe membership; an explicit option-versus-underlying
comparison; exact bounded-loss topology; every currently modeled cost plus an
explicit block for unavailable cost components; a sized firm quote for every leg;
finite Greek, tail, margin, correlation, event, liquidity, and gap exposure; and
formal invalidation, management, exit, exercise, and assignment rules. Any failed
gate creates a content-addressed abstention record.

For paper execution, record the adverse-side price plus frozen slippage and fees.
Do not label it filled until a later timestamped firm quote for every leg crosses the
frozen limit within the package-skew allowance. If no valid cross occurs before the
deadline, record `missed`; do not delete the opportunity. A local ticket or observation
remains simulated, unverified, and profitability-ineligible. A separate signed
forward-recorder record may attest the exact point-in-time input and record hashes for
prospective evaluation; it does not rewrite or self-certify the local artifact.

During a prospective episode, append state and decisions without rewriting or
widening risk. Require an independent recorder signature for every decision, every
filled-or-missed options execution observation, and every settlement, plus a distinct
outcome-source signature for each settled outcome. An options decision has exactly one
execution observation and an abstention has none; settlement is bound to that identity.
At settlement, score forecast, structure, sizing, entry, execution, management,
compliance, outcome, and luck separately. Attribution must reconcile direction,
Greeks, volatility, skew, term structure, carry, fees, slippage, assignment,
exercise, and residual.

Before any promotion evaluation, require all 11 exact negative controls and all 70
required stress scenarios. At L5, compare complete-cost net utility as a paper-bankroll
fraction against every required matched baseline. Infer lower bounds from market-session
clusters, apply the session-clustered Brier upper bound to a binary positive-net-utility
target, and retain missed fills with zero candidate utility.

## 6. Advantage-player lessons

The transferable principles are selective table choice, price shopping, bankroll
survival, dependent-risk control, process scoring, immutable record keeping, capacity
awareness, and automatic response to edge degradation. They map to liquidity and
regime abstention, option-versus-underlying comparison, conservative fractional
sizing under hard caps, portfolio Greek and tail limits, later fair-value witnesses,
and behavioral circuit breakers.

The non-transferable shortcuts are outcome worship, full Kelly on a noisy point
estimate, martingale recovery, hot-hand sizing, forced action, and treating nominal
position count as diversification. No sports-wagering capability is present.

## 7. Adversarial findings

The red teams found unsafe legacy assumptions around generic defined-risk detection,
visible holdouts, date fallbacks, truncatable ledgers, ineffective negative controls,
quote-to-fill conflation, assignment heuristics, incomplete nonlinear risk, and
partial behavioral enforcement. Ascendancy v1 repairs the contract and promotion
bypasses inside this lab. It does not certify the legacy pricing, surface, fill,
assignment, or strategy implementations; those remain isolated behind abstention.

Unresolved external blockers are qualifying point-in-time market data; five independent
promotion, evaluator, forward-recorder, outcome-source, and policy-required anchor signer
roles and key custody; the mandatory deployment root outside the repository; clean-room
replication; a lifecycle-grade American option
model with point-in-time dividends/rates/borrow; synchronized package quotes; and
prospective settlements. The static protected manifest covers immutable source and
configuration; mutable registries, ledgers, local heads, and sealed-budget state are
audited separately. Local hashes and repository writers are consistency controls, not
ACLs, OS/process boundaries, or rollback-resistant external custody. Signature
verification establishes possession of a pinned key, not organizational independence.
Every repository trust configuration currently reports `not_configured`, and no external
deployment root was supplied, so L0 is the only available evidence state.

## 8. No-edge map

No advantage is demonstrated for any of the 26 registered approach families.
Particularly hard blocks apply to 0DTE, undefined-risk premium selling, calendars,
dispersion, dynamic hedging, American assignment-sensitive structures, capacity,
and any setup lacking timestamped firm quotes. This is scoped absence of evidence,
not evidence that every possible options edge is absent.

## 9. Shadow-test plan

For any future candidate: freeze code, specification, environment, data manifest,
cost model, evaluator version, and trial count. Pass chronological development,
economic realism, six independent red-team domains, signed promotion stages,
clean-room replication, and one pinned-key, aggregate-only signed sealed verdict with
signed evaluator time before shadow eligibility. Then record at least 100 immutable
forward decisions and 100 settlements across at least 20 distinct market sessions.
Every options decision requires exactly one signed filled-or-missed execution observation;
abstentions require none. Every decision, observation, and settlement requires an
independent recorder receipt, and each outcome also requires a distinct outcome-source
receipt. Evaluate complete-cost utility against all required matched baselines with
session-clustered utility and calibration bounds. Synthetic, replay, backtest, and
model-only records do not count.

Quarantine immediately on a static-manifest or protected-source-coverage mismatch,
dynamic ledger/anchor/budget inconsistency, external-anchor mismatch, data or quote
provenance or signature failure, signer/key identity conflict, negative-control
anomaly, incomplete settlement, specification drift, calibration degradation, cost
or liquidity break, drawdown breach, risk-limit breach, or behavioral circuit
breaker. All four repository trust configurations are currently `not_configured` and the
mandatory external deployment root is not supplied, so collection cannot qualify today.
L5 remains paper shadow only.
