# Failure Envelope

Ascendancy v1 is expected to abstain outside liquid regular-session US-listed equity
and ETF options; whenever point-in-time membership, firm NBBO, quote sizes, causal
timestamps, complete-chain status, contract adjustments, dividends, rates, borrow,
or later witnesses are missing; and whenever higher Greeks or tail exposure cannot
be computed.

Uncertified or prohibited zones include American exercise and assignment complexity,
multi-leg partial fills, package routing, venue outages, calendars, diagonals,
dispersion, dynamic hedging, capacity claims, undefined-risk selling, 0DTE, and
regimes absent from the six-session development inventory. In these zones the
expected output is a reason-coded abstention, not an extrapolated price or readiness
claim.
