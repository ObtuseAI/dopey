# DOPEY Autonomous Threshold and Strategy Engineering Report

## Outcome

DOPEY now applies one fail-closed threshold vocabulary to new research admission, routing,
abstention, quarantine, stopping, and settlement controls. A companion strategy-engineering
laboratory converts immutable forward settlements into non-causal reverse hypotheses and then
into interpretable, evidence-parented forward challengers. Both scheduled lanes are local,
bounded, network-free, paper-only, and live-blocked.

This work does not claim a profitable algorithm, forward edge, calibration sufficiency, action
readiness, or trading authority. The current vNext ledger still has no matured settlements, so an
operational reverse cycle must report `insufficient_settled_observations` until real forward truth
exists.

## Threshold fabric

The sealed registry contains five policy families:

| Policy | Autonomous pass | Fail-closed behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data_admission` | Continue typed evidence processing | Quarantine missing provenance, schema, freshness, or completeness |
| `experiment_admission` | Admit to shadow evaluation only | Quarantine unsafe candidates; request missing evidence |
| `research_continuation` | Continue useful research | Narrow scope on missing utility; terminate low-yield or duplicative work |
| `forward_recording` | Preserve shadow-only forward observation | Expand evidence or abstain; prediction remains settlement telemetry, not readiness evidence |
| `settlement_admission` | Admit verified mature truth | Refuse unverified or early grading |

Every decision records the policy version, rule results, observed values, causal parents, missing
metrics, failed rules, authority, and a canonical decision hash. Protected boundaries may be
changed autonomously only in the stricter direction. Equality invariants cannot change. The
structural audit fails if any policy gains positive promotion or execution effect.

Continuous monitoring uses an explicitly conservative alpha-spending schedule and a
time-uniform Hoeffding union bound for bounded observations. Split-conformal abstention thresholds
are available for adequate calibration samples. The code does not claim SAFFRON, adaptive
conformal coverage, or any stronger guarantee than it implements.

## Reverse engineering

The reverse lane:

- joins only hashed point-in-time predictions to `settled_forward_outcome` records;
- rejects unsealed paper/live boundaries and non-forward causal order;
- extracts hypothesis probabilities, decomposed confidence, and precommitted agent contributions;
- tests transparent median-threshold associations with support, symbol, era, regime, and
  contested-truth counts;
- emits `association_only_replay_required`, never causal language or promotion authority;
- requires circular-shift, random-entry, held-out-era, cross-symbol, and multiplicity checks next.

Insufficient support produces `insufficient_settled_observations` rather than a fabricated rule.

## Forward engineering

The forward lane includes 15 typed primitive families and eight mechanism templates:

- crash-aware momentum;
- relative-value reversion;
- quality and estimate revisions;
- breadth-confirmed structural expansion;
- volatility and change-point transitions;
- state-conditioned multifactor timing;
- compact symbolic factor discovery;
- calibration- and risk-controlled abstention.

Candidates carry a deterministic factor program, source identities, evidence-parent identities,
regime guards, abstention conditions, falsification conditions, a complexity score, and a full
evaluation manifest. The factor language supports only present/past elementwise mean,
difference, product, clip, and threshold operators. It contains no future-data, broker, order,
position, or capital operator.

The archive is quality-diverse by mechanism, horizon, and complexity. It intentionally does not
select one scalar-score champion. Reverse associations steer future challenger expressions only
as evidence parents and retain every independent evaluation requirement.

## Research basis

Primary sources and the specific ideas adopted are encoded in
`dopey/strategy_engineering/source_registry.py`. Important sources include:

- [R&D-Agent-Quant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15155): separated research/development loops and factor-model feedback.
- [AlphaForge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18394), [AutoAlpha](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08245), and [AlphaFormer](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v328/huang26a.html): interpretable symbolic factors, evolutionary search, and diversity.
- [The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253) and [The Deflated Sharpe Ratio](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551): selection-bias defenses.
- [Time-uniform confidence sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240) and [Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814): sequential monitoring and risk-controlled abstention.
- [Pairs Trading](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/19/3/797/1646694), [Momentum Crashes](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2490306), and [Multifactor timing](https://academic.oup.com/jfec/article/24/3/nbag006/8658726): mechanism templates and failure-state guards.

Published performance numbers, external code output, and language-model equations are excluded
from DOPEY evidence. Every local challenger starts experimental and must earn its own held-out and
forward record.
