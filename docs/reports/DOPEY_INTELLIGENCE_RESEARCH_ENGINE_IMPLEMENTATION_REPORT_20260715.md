# DOPEY Intelligence Research Engine Implementation Report

## Outcome

The ecosystem-level Intelligence Research Engine is implemented as a domain-independent layer
above DOPEY market autoresearch.

Its mission is `discover_better_ways_of_discovering`. It transforms real operating
bottlenecks, contradictions, unknowns, repeated failures, capability gaps, and opportunities
into testable cognitive-method experiments. DOPEY markets are the first experimental domain;
market concepts do not exist in the universal core.

## Implemented architecture

### Scientific discovery

- typed knowledge signals and shared cognitive knowledge state;
- evidence-ranked research-question discovery;
- deterministic identities and causal evidence parents;
- explicit unknown, failure, opportunity, research, theory, and capability graphs.

### Computational creativity

Eight operators are active:

- analogy;
- inversion;
- morphological search;
- constraint inversion;
- recombination;
- counterfactual reasoning;
- first-principles reconstruction;
- abstraction.

Every output specifies an independent variable, dependent metrics, prediction, evidence lineage,
and falsification conditions. Creative output remains `EXPERIMENTAL`.

### Scientific experimentation

Every cognitive method receives:

- a precommitted current-best baseline;
- visible development tasks;
- private hidden selection;
- external generalization;
- real-task validation;
- independent replication requirements;
- reward-hacking, memorization, over-abstention, complexity, and evaluator-gaming traps.

The current DOPEY-only protocols explicitly set `cross_domain_promotion_blocked=true`.

### Cognitive promotion and theory

The protected evaluator requires at least:

- 40 visible samples;
- 40 private samples;
- 30 external samples;
- 20 real-task samples;
- three experimental domains;
- five independent replications;
- effect size of 0.03 with a positive lower confidence bound;
- nonnegative calibration improvement;
- transfer score of 0.67;
- efficiency score of 0.55;
- complexity score no greater than 0.35;
- deterministic and evaluator-independent evidence;
- no reward hacking or constitutional violation.

Qualified methods become `THEORY_CANDIDATE` autonomously with no human review. Theory creation
retains scope, replication, transfer, supporting evaluation identity, and falsification
conditions. Activation has no truth, permission, authority, readiness, or execution effect.

### Cognitive genome

Content-addressed genomes can inherit validated reasoning, research, creativity, planning,
evaluation, memory, and cognitive-organization methods. Candidates cannot self-grade or
self-activate. Truth and authority surfaces are non-evolvable.
